Lightning Around the Clock

Where lightning strikes is only half the story. Drag the clock: storms over land build through the afternoon and fade at night, but some spots over water, like Lake Maracaibo (the place with the most lightning on Earth), peak after dark instead. Click any location to see its hour-by-hour profile.

Every place on the map is shown at the same local solar hour (sun time, from longitude), so this is not one instant: it is "3 pm everywhere".
